### Summary

The VH-92A Patriot is the newest presidential transport helicopter operated by HMX-1, replacing the aging VH-3D Sea King as part of the VXX program.

### Aircraft Type
* [VH-92A Patriot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikorsky_VH-92) aircraft count as of 2021 GAO report: 9

### Trackable on Flightradar24 App?
* No

### Details

The VH-92A is a military variant of the Sikorsky S-92 commercial helicopter, heavily modified for the presidential transport mission. It entered service with HMX-1 in 2020 as part of the [VXX program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VXX), the long-running effort to replace the legacy VH-3D Sea King fleet.

Like all HMX-1 transport aircraft, the VH-92A wears the iconic green-and-white "White Tops" livery and may serve as Marine One when the President is aboard. It features advanced avionics, communications systems, and defensive countermeasures suited to the presidential transport role.

You'll often see these aircraft flying alongside the VH-60N Black Hawks, with two or three helicopters flying together to provide decoys. Only one will be carrying the President at any given time, making it impossible for outside observers to determine which helicopter Marine One is.

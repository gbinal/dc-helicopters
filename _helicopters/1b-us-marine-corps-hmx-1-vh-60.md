### Summary

The VH-60N White Hawk is a specialized presidential transport variant of the UH-60 Black Hawk, operated by HMX-1 alongside the VH-3D Sea King and VH-92A Patriot.

### Aircraft Type
* [VH-60N White Hawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikorsky_UH-60_Black_Hawk) aircraft count as of 2021 GAO report: 8

* No

### Details

The VH-60N is a heavily modified version of the [UH-60 Black Hawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikorsky_UH-60_Black_Hawk) configured for executive and presidential transport. HMX-1 operates these aircraft alongside the larger VH-3D Sea Kings and the newer VH-92A Patriots. The VH-60N is smaller than the VH-3D, making it well-suited for shorter hops and situations where a smaller aircraft footprint is desirable.

Like all HMX-1 VIP transport helicopters, the VH-60N features the signature green-and-white "White Tops" livery. When the President is aboard any of these aircraft, it is designated Marine One.

You'll often see two or three of these aircraft flying in formation with other HMX-1 helicopters, providing decoys to obscure which helicopter the President is traveling in.

### Viewing Tips

See the [HMX-1 Squadron page](/helicopters/1-us-marine-corps-hmx-1-squadron/) for detailed viewing tips, including where to spot these aircraft loitering over DC, flying to/from Andrews Air Force Base, and taking off or landing at the White House.
